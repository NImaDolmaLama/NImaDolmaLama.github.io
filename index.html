Frequently Asked Questions

What graphic design services do you offer?

I offer a full range of creative services: logo design, brand identity, Adobe Illustrator vector art, Photoshop photo editing, 2D animation with Adobe Animate, product packaging design, and social media poster design. If you have a specific requirement, feel free to reach out.

Are you open for new design projects?

Yes — I'm open for work and ready to take on new design projects from clients worldwide. You can contact me via the form above, email me at www.nimalama.com@gmail.com, or DM me on Instagram.

Where are you based, and do you work with international clients?

I'm based in Dharan, Nepal, and I work remotely with clients across the globe. Distance is no barrier — all deliverables are provided digitally, making collaboration seamless regardless of your location.

What design tools do you use professionally?

I work with industry-standard software: Adobe Photoshop for photo editing and digital painting, Adobe Illustrator for vector illustrations and scalable designs, Adobe Animate for 2D animation and interactive Adobe Animate projects, and Canva for rapid poster and social media design.

How many projects have you delivered?

Since starting in 2022, I have successfully delivered 97+ design projects for 50+ happy clients, ranging from startups needing a logo to established businesses refreshing their complete brand identity.

How do I hire you for a design project?

Simply fill out the contact form on this page with your project details — the type of design service you need, your timeline, and any references or inspiration. I'll get back to you within 24 hours to discuss next steps.

What is your design process and project timeline?

Every project starts with a briefing session where you share your goals, references, and deadline. I then provide a clear quote and begin the creative phase with drafts and revisions. Most projects — from logo design to full brand identity — are delivered within 3 to 10 business days depending on scope. Need a rush delivery? Get a free quote and let's discuss your timeline.
